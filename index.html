How it works

Section titled “How it works”

Laya (local)

A small local model served by laya.cpp makes each click and keystroke from the elements it observes on the page.

Pilot (multimodal)

A multimodal model runs the spec: it sends intents, checks results, marks steps, records findings, reviews screenshots, and speaks short progress updates.

Orchestrator (you or an agent)

Starts sessions and specs, signs in, handles checkpoints such as database or mail proof, and reviews the verdicts.

Guards

Commit buttons such as Save or Delete need an explicit intent, only stated values are typed, and loops or stalls end in a checkpoint. Tokens and codes are masked.
